# Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Internship

## Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn in a telecommunications company using the **Telco Customer Churn dataset**. The main objective is to understand customer retention patterns, identify factors associated with customer churn, and discover customer groups with higher churn rates using Python-based data analysis and visualization.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer churn and retention rates.
- Understand customer tenure and monthly charges.
- Compare churn across different contract types.
- Analyze internet services and payment methods.
- Explore the relationship between technical support and customer churn.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 📂 Dataset
The project uses the **Telco Customer Churn dataset**, which contains customer information, subscribed services, account details, payment information, and churn status.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python** – Data analysis
- **Google Colab** – Development environment
- **Pandas** – Data manipulation and analysis
- **NumPy** – Numerical computations
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. Loaded and explored the dataset.
2. Checked missing values, duplicates, and data types.
3. Cleaned the data and handled missing values.
4. Calculated customer churn and retention rates.
5. Analyzed customer tenure and monthly charges.
6. Compared churn across contract types, internet services, payment methods, and customer characteristics.
7. Created graphs and visualizations to understand churn patterns.
8. Developed business recommendations based on the analysis.

## 📊 Key Analysis
- Overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Customer churn by contract type.
- Churn patterns across internet services and payment methods.
- Customer tenure analysis.
- Comparison of monthly charges between customer groups.
- Relationship between technical support and customer churn.
- Analysis of customer characteristics associated with churn.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve onboarding and customer support for new customers.
- Offer suitable long-term plans to customers who may benefit from them.
- Monitor customer feedback and resolve service-related issues.
- Review pricing strategies and payment experiences.
- Track churn rates regularly to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Develop targeted customer retention strategies based on the analysis.

## 🏁 Conclusion
This project uses **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and data visualization to understand customer churn and retention patterns in the telecommunications industry. The analysis helps identify customer groups that may require additional support and provides actionable recommendations to improve customer satisfaction and retention.
